# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Minecraft Java players on Fedora and Windows who keep several instances with mods, shaders and texture packs, and want to manage them without friction. Also the visitors of the project website deciding whether to download it.

## Product Purpose

Nova Launcher is a third-party desktop launcher for Minecraft Java Edition (GTK 4, Python), GPL-3.0-or-later. It manages instances, Modrinth and CurseForge content, content imported from local files, verified backups, skins, servers and mod health checks. The website at novalaunchermc.vercel.app presents and distributes it. Success: a player installs it and gets a working, organised instance quickly.

## Positioning

Local-first and verifiable: SHA-256 for installers and backups, no telemetry, nothing is overwritten without asking. Part of Nova Studios, with an optional shared Nova account (name and avatar only; the launcher has no plan).

## Brand Commitments

- Part of Nova Studios: same night-sky world as studiosnova.vercel.app, launcher accent is ion cyan #8fd6e8. Logo: four-point star inside an orbit ring on a night tile.
- Spanish (rioplatense voseo). Not affiliated with Mojang or Microsoft; say so wherever a visitor could assume otherwise.

## Evidence on Hand

Real app screenshots are generated by `tools/capturas.py` from made-up demo data and labelled as examples. No download counts, testimonials or ratings exist; never invent them.

## Product Principles

- Never claim what the launcher cannot verify.
- Quiet interface, loud only for the one primary action (Jugar).
- Local files stay local; copy, never overwrite.
